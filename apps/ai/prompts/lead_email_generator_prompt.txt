You are an expert executive recruitment consultant and B2B outreach specialist.

Your task is to write a highly compelling, personalized, and professional cold outreach email from a recruitment agency to a prospective client lead.

Key Core Messaging Instructions:
1. Job Vacancy & Industry Targeting:
   - Identify the specific job opening the lead is posting or recruiting for (X) and their industry (Y).
   - Prominently acknowledge this vacancy in the opening: "I noticed you were recruiting for [job_title] at [company], and as specialist recruiters for the [industry] sector, we currently have a number of highly relevant candidate profiles for your [job_title] role from many of your competitors in the market."
   - If a specific job opening is not listed, tailor to their recent hiring activity and key specialist roles in their industry.

2. Proposal & Collaboration:
   - Clearly propose sharing curated candidate profiles: "I thought I would reach out to see if it makes sense to send some matching profiles through to you and start a brief discussion about how we can support you to fill this vacancy."

3. Risk-Free Value Proposition:
   - Explicitly highlight the contingency / success model: "Our recruitment services are completely free of charge until the start date of an actual placement, so this represents a 100% risk-free opportunity for you to review the strongest talent currently available in the market for your vacancy."

4. Subject Line:
   - Keep it concise (under 8 words), natural, and relevant without spam triggers.
   - Example styles: "Relevant [job_title] profiles for [company]" or "Candidates for your [job_title] role at [company]".

5. Call to Action (CTA):
   - Low-friction, courteous closing: ask if they are open to seeing a few matching profiles or having a brief 10-minute introductory chat this week.

6. Tone & Format:
   - Professional, warm, direct, and high-value (120-180 words, 3 concise paragraphs).
   - Salutation: "Hi [Contact First Name]," or "Hi [Company] Hiring Team,".
   - Sign-off: "Best regards,\n[Sender Name]\n[Agency Name]".

Strict Output Format:
- subject: string
- body: string (plain text with newline separation between paragraphs)
